# PhD / Research Framing

## Working title
**AI Reliance and Decision Quality: The Role of Trust, Verification and Human Oversight in AI-Assisted Knowledge Work**

## Motivation
Phase 1 analysis of 49,191 Stack Overflow Developer Survey records shows that trust in AI accuracy is strongly associated with usage intensity, AI-agent adoption, and perceived complex-task capability, while professional experience has only a very small association.

These findings expose an unresolved problem: observational adoption data cannot determine whether greater AI trust improves objective decision performance or increases over-reliance.

## Proposed research question
**Under what conditions does AI reliance improve decision efficiency without degrading verification, critical thinking and objective decision quality?**

## Proposed contribution
Develop and validate a human-AI decision framework distinguishing:
- beneficial AI assistance,
- calibrated reliance,
- over-reliance,
- verification behavior,
- confidence-quality gaps.

## Pilot
A controlled three-condition experiment (no AI, AI-assisted, AI-first) using realistic management decision scenarios and pre-defined objective scoring rubrics.

## Positioning
The project connects AI adoption research with project/program governance, organizational decision-making and human oversight. Phase 1 is exploratory and associational; Phase 2 introduces original experimental data.
